The Alchemist's Story

Meet Keketso
"The Alchemist" Leu

Born in Westonaria in 1994 and raised in Matatiele, Keketso Leu grew up watching South African businesses struggle to compete in an increasingly digital world — not because of lack of ambition, but lack of access to world-class digital craft.

That observation became an obsession. After years honing skills in full-stack development, UI/UX design, and digital strategy — delivering real projects for South African businesses — Keketso founded The Tech Alchemy Lab with a single mission: to give African businesses the same premium digital weapons that global giants use.

Case Study

Bambanani Day Care

A professional website for a registered daycare that builds parent trust, improves usability, and supports fast local discovery.

Client Bambanani Day Care
Industry Education
Focus Trust, mobile performance, SEO
Visit Website

Client overview

Registered daycare and aftercare centre needed a polished digital front door that reassures parents and simplifies enquiry.

The challenge

Parents needed fast access to boarding information, enrolment steps, contact details and visual proof of the centre’s professionalism.

Results

A modern website designed for speed, clarity and conversion. The site presents a strong parent-focused story with clear action paths.

The challenge

Young families needed confidence before they visited the centre. The existing online presence was dated, slow and unclear on enrollment procedures.

My solution

  • Designed a clean parent-first homepage with trust signals, programmes and FAQs.
  • Built a mobile-first experience that works quickly on low-bandwidth devices.
  • Structured content for local SEO and contact inquiries with visible call-to-actions.
  • Implemented a polished gallery and clear enrollment sections so parents can decide fast.

Technology stack

Next.js React SEO Responsive

Design process

  1. Research and parent journey mapping
  2. Information architecture and content hierarchy
  3. Visual design and performance-first implementation
Desktop preview

Homepage with fast-loading layouts and trust-driven content.

Mobile view

Mobile enrollment flow designed for busy parents.

Information gallery

Clear service sections for daycare, aftercare and contact.

Business impact

Delivered a website that positions Bambanani Day Care as a professional, modern and trustworthy option for parents. The refreshed presence makes enquiries easier and supports local search discovery.

Lessons learned

For education brands, confidence is earned through clarity. Fast loading pages, simple enrollment flows and proof points keep busy parents moving toward contact.
