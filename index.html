The Alchemist's Story

Meet Keketso
"The Alchemist" Leu

Born and raised in Johannesburg, Keketso Leu grew up watching South African businesses struggle to compete in an increasingly digital world — not because of lack of ambition, but lack of access to world-class digital craft.

That observation became an obsession. After years honing skills in full-stack development, UI/UX design, and digital strategy — working on projects across South Africa, Europe, and the US — Keketso founded The Tech Alchemy Lab with a single mission: to give African businesses the same premium digital weapons that global giants use.
